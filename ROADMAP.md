## 📚 Phase 1: BSc Foundation (Year 1, Months 1-12)

### Month 1-2: PHYS 2124 Mathematical Methods I
**Focus:** Vector calculus, ODEs, Linear algebra, Complex analysis
**Resources:** MIT OCW 18.02 + 18.03 + Boas
**Deliverables:**
- Python vector calculus toolkit
- ODE solvers (Euler, RK4, scipy)
- 50+ practice problems solved
- Anki deck (100+ cards)

### Month 3-4: PHYS 2125 Classical Mechanics
**Focus:** Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, Central force, Oscillations
**Resources:** MIT OCW 8.01 + 8.02 + Taylor
**Deliverables:**
- Lagrangian/Hamiltonian simulator
- N-body simulation
- Pendulum + chaotic system visualization
- 40+ problems

### Month 5-6: PHYS 2126 Electromagnetism
**Focus:** Maxwell equations, EM waves, Radiation
**Resources:** MIT OCW 8.07 + Griffiths
**Deliverables:**
- Numerical FDTD Maxwell solver
- Field visualization (matplotlib + plotly)
- Antenna radiation pattern
- 40+ problems

### Month 7-8: PHYS 2127 Quantum Mechanics I
**Focus:** Schrödinger equation, Hydrogen atom, Harmonic oscillator
**Resources:** MIT OCW 8.04 + 8.05 + Griffiths QM
**Deliverables:**
- 1D Schrödinger solver
- Hydrogen orbital visualization
- Quantum tunneling simulation
- 40+ problems

### Month 9-10: PHYS 2128 Quantum Mechanics II
**Focus:** Perturbation theory, Variational method, Spin
**Resources:** Sakurai + Griffiths QM
**Deliverables:**
- Perturbation theory calculator
- Variational method for He atom
- Spin dynamics simulation
- 30+ problems

### Month 11-12: Statistical Mechanics
**Focus:** Ensembles, Phase transitions, Critical phenomena
**Resources:** MIT OCW 8.08 + Pathria
**Deliverables:**
- Ising model Monte Carlo
- Phase transition visualization
- Partition function calculator
- 30+ problems
