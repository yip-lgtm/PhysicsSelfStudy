## 🎯 Program Overview

**All students in the MSc Physics program are required to take this course.** Seminars and small group tutorials expose students to:

- Cutting-edge physics research
- Faculty research areas
- Industry applications of physics
- Career paths (academic, industry, government)

---

## 📋 Components

### 1. **Departmental Seminars**
- Weekly departmental colloquia
- Invited speakers (local + international)
- Q&A with speakers
- Networking opportunities

### 2. **Faculty Research Talks**
- HKUST Physics faculty present their research
- Identify potential PhD supervisors
- Understand current projects in department

### 3. **Tutorials / Small Group Discussions**
- Faculty-led tutorials
- Discussion of research papers
- Small group problem-solving

### 4. **Student Presentations**
- Present a research paper
- Peer feedback
- Q&A

### 5. **Career Panels**
- Academic career path
- Industry physics careers
- Government / national lab
- Entrepreneurship in physics

---

## 📋 Requirements

- Attendance at all seminars (or make-up requirements)
- Active participation in tutorials
- 1-2 paper presentations per term
- Final reflection (varies by year)

---

## 🎯 Skills Developed

- Research awareness
- Critical thinking about current literature
- Networking
- Public speaking
- Q&A in academic setting
