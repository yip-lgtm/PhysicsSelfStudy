## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Survey of **contemporary research frontiers** in physics, covering topics from recent literature and active research areas.

### Sample Topics

1. **Condensed Matter**
   - Topological materials
   - Quantum materials
   - 2D materials

2. **Quantum Information**
   - Quantum computing
   - Quantum cryptography

3. **Astrophysics & Cosmology**
   - Dark matter, dark energy
   - Gravitational waves

4. **Particle Physics**
   - Standard Model and beyond
   - Neutrino physics

5. **Energy Materials**
   - Photovoltaics
   - Battery technology
