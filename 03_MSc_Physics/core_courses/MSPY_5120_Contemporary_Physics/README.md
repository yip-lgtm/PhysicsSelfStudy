## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Survey of **contemporary research frontiers** in physics, covering topics from recent literature and active research areas.

---

## 📚 Sample Topics (Rotating)

### 1. **Condensed Matter**
- Topological materials (TIs, Weyl semimetals)
- Quantum materials
- 2D materials (graphene, TMDs)
- Strongly correlated systems
- Superconductivity (high-Tc, unconventional)
- Quantum spin liquids
- Twistronics (magic-angle graphene)

### 2. **Quantum Information**
- Quantum computing platforms (superconducting, trapped ion, photonic)
- Quantum cryptography (QKD)
- Quantum networks
- Quantum advantage demonstrations
- Quantum error correction

### 3. **Astrophysics & Cosmology**
- Dark matter, dark energy
- Gravitational waves (LIGO, LISA)
- Multi-messenger astronomy
- CMB observations
- Black hole imaging (EHT)

### 4. **Particle Physics**
- Standard Model and beyond
- Neutrino physics (mass, oscillations)
- Higgs boson
- LHC experiments
- Dark matter direct detection

### 5. **Energy Materials**
- Photovoltaics (perovskite, organic)
- Battery technology (Li-ion, solid-state)
- Hydrogen storage
- Catalysis for sustainability
- Superconducting magnets

### 6. **Soft Matter, Biological Physics**
- Active matter
- Liquid crystals
- Protein folding
- Cellular mechanics
