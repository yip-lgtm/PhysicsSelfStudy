## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Foundations**
- Macrostates vs microstates
- Phase space
- Liouville's theorem
- Ergodicity
- Ensembles (microcanonical, canonical, grand canonical)

### 2. **Classical Statistical Mechanics**
- Boltzmann distribution
- Partition function
- Free energy
- Ideal gas (Maxwell-Boltzmann)
- Equipartition theorem
- Maxwell velocity distribution

### 3. **Quantum Statistical Mechanics**
- **Fermi-Dirac statistics** (electrons in metals)
- **Bose-Einstein statistics** (photons, phonons)
- Planck blackbody radiation
- Bose-Einstein condensation
- Debye model of solids

### 4. **Thermodynamics from Stat Mech**
- Internal energy
- Entropy (Boltzmann, Gibbs)
- Temperature, pressure
- Chemical potential
- Phase transitions (intro)

### 5. **Applications**
- Paramagnetism, ferromagnetism (Ising)
- Lattice vibrations
- Blackbody radiation
- Chemical equilibrium
- Phase diagrams
