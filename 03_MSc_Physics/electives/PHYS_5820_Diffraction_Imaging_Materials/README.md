## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **X-ray Diffraction (XRD)**
- Bragg's law
- Laue conditions
- Powder diffraction
- Single-crystal XRD
- Rietveld refinement
- Indexing patterns

### 2. **Electron Diffraction**
- LEED (low-energy electron diffraction)
- RHEED (reflection high-energy)
- TEM diffraction
- Kikuchi patterns
- Convergent beam electron diffraction (CBED)

### 3. **Neutron Diffraction**
- Differences from X-ray
- Magnetic structure determination
- Powder neutron diffraction

### 4. **Electron Microscopy**
- **SEM** (scanning electron microscopy)
  - Imaging modes (SE, BSE)
  - EDX (energy-dispersive X-ray)
  - EBSD (electron backscatter diffraction)
- **TEM** (transmission electron microscopy)
  - Bright/dark field imaging
  - HRTEM (high-resolution)
  - STEM
  - EELS (electron energy loss)
  - Tomography

### 5. **Scanning Probe Microscopy**
- **STM** (scanning tunneling)
- **AFM** (atomic force)
  - Contact, tapping, non-contact
  - MFM (magnetic force)
  - KPFM (Kelvin probe)
  - PFM (piezoelectric)

### 6. **Image Analysis**
- Digital micrograph processing
- FFT analysis
- Lattice imaging
- Defect analysis
