## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Quantum Materials Fundamentals**
- Band theory review
- Topological insulators
- 2D materials
- Superconductors
- Weyl/Dirac semimetals

### 2. **Quantum Devices**
- Quantum dots
- Single-electron transistors
- Quantum Hall effect devices
- Josephson junctions
- SQUIDs

### 3. **Quantum Sensors**
- Atom interferometers
- Quantum-enhanced magnetometers
- NV centers in diamond
- Superconducting qubits
- Trapped ions

### 4. **Quantum Technologies**
- Quantum computing hardware
- Quantum cryptography (QKD)
- Quantum communication
- Quantum metrology
- Quantum sensing applications
