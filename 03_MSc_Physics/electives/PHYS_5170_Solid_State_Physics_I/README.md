## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Crystal Structure**
- Bravais lattices
- Reciprocal lattice
- Brillouin zone
- Crystal structures (FCC, BCC, HCP, diamond)
- X-ray diffraction

### 2. **Electronic Properties**
- Free electron gas
- Bloch's theorem
- Nearly-free electron model
- Tight-binding model
- Band structure
- Metals, insulators, semiconductors

### 3. **Semiconductor Physics**
- Effective mass
- Holes
- Carrier statistics
- pn junctions
- Heterostructures

### 4. **Lattice Dynamics**
- Phonons
- Acoustic + optical modes
- Dispersion relations
- Specific heat (Einstein, Debye models)
- Thermal conductivity

### 5. **Magnetic Properties**
- Diamagnetism, paramagnetism
- Ferromagnetism, antiferromagnetism
- Magnetic ordering
- Mean-field theory (Weiss)

## 🎯 Project Ideas

- Compute band structure of Si, Ge, GaAs (tight-binding)
- Calculate phonon dispersion in 1D diatomic chain
- Simulate ferromagnet phase transition (Ising model)
- Plot Fermi surface of Cu (free electron + pseudopotential)
