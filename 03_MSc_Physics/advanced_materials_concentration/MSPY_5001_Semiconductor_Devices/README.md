## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Semiconductor Physics**
- Crystal structure, band structure
- Carrier transport (drift, diffusion)
- Generation, recombination
- pn junctions (depletion, I-V)
- Bipolar transistors (BJT)

### 2. **MOS Devices**
- **MOSFET** structure
- Threshold voltage
- I-V characteristics
- **CMOS** technology
- Scaling (Moore's Law, Dennard)
- Short-channel effects
- FinFET, GAA

### 3. **Memory & Sensors**
- **DRAM** (capacitor-based)
- **SRAM** (6T, 4T)
- **Flash** (NOR, NAND)
- Emerging memories (MRAM, RRAM, PCM)
- Image sensors (CCD, CMOS)
- **MEMS** devices
- Power devices (IGBT, SiC, GaN)

### 4. **Processing**
- **Oxidation** (dry, wet)
- **Diffusion**
- **Ion implantation**
- **Lithography** (DUV, EUV)
- **Etching** (dry, wet)
- **Deposition** (CVD, PVD, ALD)
- **Cleanroom** procedures
- **Back-end-of-line** (BEOL)
