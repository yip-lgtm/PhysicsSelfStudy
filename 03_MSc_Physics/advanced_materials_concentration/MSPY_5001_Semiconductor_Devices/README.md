## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Semiconductor Physics**
- Crystal structure, band structure
- Carrier transport
- pn junctions
- Bipolar transistors

### 2. **MOS Devices**
- MOSFET structure
- CMOS technology
- Scaling (Moore's Law)
- Short-channel effects

### 3. **Memory & Sensors**
- DRAM, SRAM, Flash
- Image sensors (CCD, CMOS)
- MEMS devices
- Power devices (IGBT, SiC, GaN)

### 4. **Processing**
- Oxidation, diffusion
- Ion implantation
- Lithography
- Etching, deposition
- Cleanroom procedures
