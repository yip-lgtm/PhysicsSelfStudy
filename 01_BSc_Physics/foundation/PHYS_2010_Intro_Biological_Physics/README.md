## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Molecular Biology from Physics Perspective**
- DNA, RNA, proteins
- Molecular bonds
- Self-assembly

### 2. **Biomechanics**
- Muscle contraction
- Cell motility
- Cytoskeleton
- Flagella, cilia

### 3. **Bioelectricity**
- Neuron action potential
- Hodgkin-Huxley model
- Nernst equation
- Ion channels

### 4. **Diffusion & Transport**
- Brownian motion
- Fick's laws
- Active transport
- Random walks in biology

### 5. **Soft Matter Physics in Biology**
- Polymers (proteins, DNA)
- Membranes (lipid bilayers)
- Phase separation in cells
- Liquid-liquid phase separation

### 6. **Biophysical Techniques**
- Optical microscopy
- Fluorescence (FRET, FRAP)
- AFM for biomolecules
- Cryo-EM
- X-ray crystallography of proteins
