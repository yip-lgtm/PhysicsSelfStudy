## 🎯 Learning Objectives

A non-technical introduction to quantum information science for the curious public and non-physics majors.

### Topics Covered

1. **Quantum Weirdness**
   - Wave-particle duality
   - Superposition
   - Entanglement
   - Measurement problem

2. **Quantum Computing**
   - Qubits
   - Quantum gates
   - Quantum algorithms (Shor, Grover)
   - Quantum advantage
   - Real hardware (IBM, Google, IonQ)

3. **Quantum Cryptography**
   - BB84 protocol
   - Quantum key distribution (QKD)
   - Post-quantum cryptography

4. **Quantum Sensing**
   - Atomic clocks
   - MRI
   - Gravitational sensing

5. **Quantum Communication**
   - Quantum internet
   - Quantum repeaters
   - Quantum networks

6. **Philosophy & Implications**
   - Many-worlds interpretation
   - Quantum consciousness (debunked)
   - Reality and information
