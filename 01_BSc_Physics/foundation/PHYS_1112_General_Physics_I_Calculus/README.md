## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Same content as PHYS 1111, but with **full calculus integration from the start**. Designed for students with strong math background.

### Topics Covered

1. **Mechanics** (with full calculus)
   - Vectors, kinematics
   - Newton's laws
   - Work-energy theorem
   - Conservation of momentum, energy
   - Rotational dynamics
   - Oscillations
   - Gravity

2. **Waves**
3. **Thermodynamics (intro)**
4. **Calculus applications**
   - Derivatives, integrals
   - Vectors in 3D
   - Differential equations
