## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Companion laboratory course to PHYS 1111/1112. Hands-on experiments in classical mechanics.

### Typical Experiments

1. **Measurement & Uncertainty**
   - Error analysis
   - Significant figures
   - Propagation of errors

2. **Mechanics**
   - Free fall (g measurement)
   - Projectile motion
   - Air track collisions (momentum, energy)
   - Newton's second law (Atwood machine)
   - Simple pendulum
   - Spring oscillations (SHM)

3. **Data Analysis**
   - Linearization
   - Least-squares fitting
   - Graphing (linear, log)
   - Error bars

4. **Lab Report Writing**
   - Scientific format
   - Results, discussion
   - Conclusion
