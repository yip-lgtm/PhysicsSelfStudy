## 🎯 Learning Objectives

**Advanced honors version of PHYS 1114**. More rigorous treatment of electromagnetism, optics, and modern physics.

### Topics Covered (deeper than 1114)

1. **Electricity & Magnetism**
   - Electrostatics (vector calculus)
   - Gauss's law
   - Electric potential
   - Capacitance, dielectrics
   - Current, resistance
   - Magnetic field (Biot-Savart, Ampère)
   - Faraday's law
   - Inductance
   - Maxwell's equations (full)
   - Electromagnetic waves
   - Radiation

2. **Optics**
   - Wave optics (interference, diffraction, polarization)
   - Fourier optics
   - Coherence theory (intro)

3. **Special Relativity**
   - Lorentz transformations
   - 4-vectors
   - Relativistic dynamics
   - E=mc²
