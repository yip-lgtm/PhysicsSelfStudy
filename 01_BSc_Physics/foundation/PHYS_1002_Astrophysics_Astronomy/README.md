## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Solar System**
- Planets, moons
- Small bodies (asteroids, comets)
- Formation
- Exoplanets

### 2. **Stars**
- HR diagram
- Stellar evolution
- Supernovae
- White dwarfs, neutron stars, black holes

### 3. **Galaxies**
- Milky Way structure
- Galaxy types (spiral, elliptical, irregular)
- Galaxy evolution
- Active galactic nuclei

### 4. **Cosmology**
- Big Bang
- Cosmic microwave background
- Dark matter, dark energy
- Expansion of the universe
- Fate of the universe

### 5. **Observational Tools**
- Telescopes (optical, radio, X-ray)
- Spectroscopy
- Multi-messenger astronomy
- Space missions
