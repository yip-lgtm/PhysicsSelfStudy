## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Energy Fundamentals**
- Forms of energy
- Thermodynamics
- Energy conversion
- Efficiency, power

### 2. **Fossil Fuels**
- Coal, oil, natural gas
- Extraction, refining
- Combustion
- Environmental impact (CO2, pollution)

### 3. **Nuclear Energy**
- Fission
- Fusion
- Reactor design
- Waste management
- Safety, accidents (Chernobyl, Fukushima)

### 4. **Renewable Energy**
- **Solar** (PV, thermal)
- **Wind**
- **Hydroelectric**
- **Geothermal**
- **Biomass**
- **Tidal, wave**
- Energy storage

### 5. **Environmental Issues**
- Climate change
- Air pollution
- Water pollution
- Resource depletion
- Sustainability

### 6. **Future Energy**
- Hydrogen economy
- Smart grid
- Energy efficiency
- Carbon capture
- Policy and economics
