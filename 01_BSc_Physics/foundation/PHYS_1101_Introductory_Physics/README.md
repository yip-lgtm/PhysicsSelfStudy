## 🎯 Learning Objectives

A non-calculus-based introduction to physics for students from diverse backgrounds.

### 1. **Mechanics**
- Newton's laws
- Forces
- Energy, work, power
- Momentum, collisions
- Rotational motion
- Gravity

### 2. **Waves and Sound**
- Wave types
- Superposition
- Standing waves
- Doppler effect

### 3. **Thermodynamics**
- Temperature, heat
- First, second laws
- Heat engines, refrigerators
- Entropy

### 4. **Electricity and Magnetism**
- Electric charge, field
- Potential, capacitance
- Current, resistance
- Magnetic field, induction

### 5. **Light and Optics**
- Reflection, refraction
- Lenses, mirrors
- Interference, diffraction

### 6. **Modern Physics (intro)**
- Special relativity
- Atomic physics
- Nuclear physics
- Particle physics
