## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Introduction to **20th-century physics**: relativity, quantum mechanics, atomic/nuclear/particle physics.

### 1. **Special Relativity**
- Galilean vs Lorentz transformations
- Time dilation, length contraction
- Relativistic velocity addition
- E=mc²
- 4-vectors
- Relativistic momentum, energy

### 2. **Origins of Quantum Mechanics**
- Blackbody radiation (Planck)
- Photoelectric effect (Einstein)
- Compton scattering
- Bohr model
- de Broglie wavelength
- Davisson-Germer experiment

### 3. **Quantum Mechanics Foundations**
- Wave function
- Schrödinger equation
- Uncertainty principle
- Probability interpretation
- Operators, observables
- 1D potentials (infinite well, finite well, step, barrier)

### 4. **Atomic Physics**
- Hydrogen atom (Schrödinger solution)
- Angular momentum, spin
- Pauli exclusion
- Multi-electron atoms
- Periodic table
- Atomic spectroscopy

### 5. **Nuclear & Particle Physics (intro)**
- Nuclear structure
- Radioactivity (alpha, beta, gamma)
- Nuclear reactions, fission, fusion
- Standard Model (quarks, leptons, bosons)
- Fundamental interactions
