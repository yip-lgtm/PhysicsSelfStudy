## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Continuation of PHYS 1111, covering electromagnetism, optics, and modern physics.

### 1. **Electricity & Magnetism**
- Electrostatics (Coulomb, Gauss's law)
- Electric potential, capacitance
- Current, resistance, Ohm's law
- DC circuits
- Magnetic field, force
- Ampère's law, Faraday's law
- Inductance
- AC circuits
- Maxwell's equations

### 2. **Optics**
- Wave nature of light
- Geometric optics (reflection, refraction, lenses, mirrors)
- Interference (Young's double slit)
- Diffraction (single, multiple slits)
- Polarization

### 3. **Modern Physics (intro)**
- Special relativity
- Photons, photoelectric effect
- Bohr model, de Broglie
- Wave-particle duality
- Atomic structure
- Nuclear physics
