## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Companion laboratory course to PHYS 1114. Experiments in electromagnetism, optics, and modern physics.

### Typical Experiments

1. **Electricity**
   - Ohm's law, resistor networks
   - Capacitance, RC circuits
   - Inductance, LR circuits

2. **Magnetism**
   - Magnetic field of a coil
   - Earth's magnetic field
   - Force on current-carrying wire

3. **Optics**
   - Lens focal length
   - Telescope, microscope
   - Interference (Michelson, Fabry-Perot)
   - Diffraction (single, double slit)
   - Polarization

4. **Modern Physics**
   - Photoelectric effect
   - e/m ratio of electron
   - Atomic spectroscopy
