## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. **Electrodynamics**
   - Faraday's law
   - Maxwell's equations (full)
   - EM wave equation
   - Energy and momentum of EM fields

2. **Electromagnetic Waves**
   - Plane waves in vacuum
   - Plane waves in media
   - Reflection, refraction
   - Polarization

3. **Radiation**
   - Retarded potentials
   - Liénard-Wiechert potentials
   - Electric dipole radiation
   - Antenna theory

4. **Relativistic Electrodynamics**
   - Special relativity review
   - 4-vectors, 4-tensors
   - Covariant formulation of E&M
   - Lorentz transformations of E, B
