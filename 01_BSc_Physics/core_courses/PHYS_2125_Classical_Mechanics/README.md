---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. **Newtonian Mechanics**
   - Forces, energy, momentum
   - Conservation laws
   - Oscillations (SHM, damped, forced)

2. **Lagrangian Mechanics**
   - Generalized coordinates
   - Euler-Lagrange equations
   - Constraints

3. **Hamiltonian Mechanics**
   - Hamilton's equations
   - Phase space
   - Liouville's theorem

4. **Central Force Problem**
   - Kepler problem
   - Effective potential
   - Orbital mechanics

5. **Rigid Body Dynamics**
   - Rotation matrices
   - Moment of inertia
   - Euler's equations

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1-2: Newtonian Mechanics Review
- Newton's laws, energy, momentum
- Oscillations (SHM, damped, forced, resonance)
- Driven oscillator with damping

### Week 3-4: Lagrangian Mechanics
- Generalized coordinates
- Constraints (holonomic, non-holonomic)
- Euler-Lagrange equations
- Applications (pendulum, Atwood machine)

### Week 5-6: Hamiltonian Mechanics
- Hamilton's principle
- Phase space
- Poisson brackets
- Liouville's theorem

### Week 7-8: Central Force Problem
- Effective potential
- Kepler problem
- Orbital mechanics
- Scattering
