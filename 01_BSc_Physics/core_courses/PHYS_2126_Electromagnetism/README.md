---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. **Electrostatics**
   - Coulomb's law, Gauss's law
   - Electric potential
   - Poisson and Laplace equations
   - Capacitance, dielectrics

2. **Magnetostatics**
   - Biot-Savart law
   - Ampere's law
   - Magnetic vector potential
   - Inductance

3. **Maxwell's Equations**
   - Displacement current
   - Full Maxwell equations
   - Boundary conditions

4. **Electromagnetic Waves**
   - Wave equation
   - Plane waves in vacuum and media
   - Polarization
   - Reflection, refraction

5. **Radiation**
   - Dipole radiation
   - Antenna theory
   - Scattering
