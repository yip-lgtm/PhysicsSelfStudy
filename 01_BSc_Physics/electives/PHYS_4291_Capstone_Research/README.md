## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** PHYS 4291
- **Name:** Capstone Research
- **University:** HKUST

- **Phase:** 1 (BSc Capstone)
- **Credits:** 6 (likely)
- **Duration:** 1-2 semesters

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

A **research-based capstone experience** for final-year BSc Physics students. Students work on an independent research project under faculty supervision.

---

## 📋 Project Structure

### 1. **Project Selection**
- Survey HKUST Physics faculty research
- Discuss potential projects with supervisors
- Submit project proposal
- Get approval from department

### 2. **Research Activities**
- Literature review (10-20 papers)
- Methodology design
- Lab work / computation / theory
- Regular meetings with supervisor
- Progress reports

### 3. **Deliverables**
- **Research report** (40-60 pages)
- **Oral presentation** (15-20 min)
- **Q&A session**
- **Poster presentation** (department research day)

### 4. **Assessment**
- Supervisor evaluation (50%)
- Research output quality (30%)
- Report + presentation (20%)

---

## 🎯 Project Categories

### 1. **Experimental**
- Build measurement apparatus
- Conduct experiments
- Analyze data
- Compare with theory

### 2. **Computational**
- Implement physics model
- Run simulations
- Compare with data
- HPC usage

### 3. **Theoretical**
- Derive new physics
- Analytical calculations
- Numerical validation
- Predictions

### 4. **Data-Driven / ML**
- Apply ML to physics data
- Develop new methods
- Physics-informed neural networks

---

## 🎯 Research Areas @ HKUST Physics

- Astrophysics (observational, theoretical)
- Condensed matter (topological, 2D, strongly correlated)
- Quantum information
- Particle physics
- Soft matter, biophysics
- Atomic, molecular, optical (AMO)

---

## 🎯 Project Examples

- "Topological edge states in twisted bilayer graphene"
- "Machine learning for cosmic ray classification"
- "Quantum simulation of the Fermi-Hubbard model"
- "Fabrication of perovskite solar cells"
