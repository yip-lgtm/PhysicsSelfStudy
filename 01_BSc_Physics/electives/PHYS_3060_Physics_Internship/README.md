- **Code:** PHYS 3060
- **Name:** Physics Internship
- **University:** HKUST

- **Credits:** 3

---

## 🎯 Program Overview

A **summer internship** with a host organization (industry, research lab, or company) for at least **8 weeks**. Students apply physics knowledge in a real-world setting.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. **Practical Application**
   - Apply physics knowledge to industry problems
   - Develop professional skills
   - Build industry network

2. **Career Development**
   - Explore career paths
   - Resume building
   - Interview experience

3. **Reporting**
   - Mid-term report
   - Final presentation
   - Supervisor evaluation

---

## 📋 Eligibility

- Year 2 or above standing
- Cumulative GPA ≥ 2.5
- Approval from physics department

---

## 🏢 Sample Host Organizations

- HKUST research labs
- Hong Kong Observatory
- Hospital Authority (medical physics)
- Industry R&D departments
- International research institutes
