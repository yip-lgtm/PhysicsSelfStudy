### What are X-courses?
"X" denotes **self-directed / research-style courses** with variable credit (1-3 per semester). They are typically:
- 1-on-1 with a faculty supervisor
- Focused on a specific research topic
- Result in a final report or presentation
- Good preparation for **PhD applications**

- 1 credit each
- Build research awareness + network
- Required for the BSc Physics major
