## 🎯 Learning Objectives

1. **Particle Physics**
   - Quarks, leptons
   - Gauge bosons
   - Standard Model
   - Higgs mechanism

2. **Symmetries and Conservation**
   - Noether's theorem
   - CPT theorem
   - Isospin, strangeness

3. **Experimental Methods**
   - Particle accelerators
   - Detectors
   - Cross sections

4. **Cosmology**
   - Big Bang
   - Cosmic microwave background
   - Dark matter, dark energy
   - Baryon asymmetry
