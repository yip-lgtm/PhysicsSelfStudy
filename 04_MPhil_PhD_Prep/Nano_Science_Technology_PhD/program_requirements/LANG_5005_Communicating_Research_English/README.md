- **Code:** LANG 5005

- **Recommended:** Complete in **first year of study**

---

## 🎯 Course Description

This course aims to help research postgraduate students to develop skills they need to **understand how to successfully communicate research in English** to:

- **Academic** audiences
- **Cross-disciplinary** audiences
- **Non-specialist** audiences

---

## 📋 Topics Covered

- Academic writing (papers, thesis)
- Conference presentations
- Poster design
- Research communication
- Grant proposal writing
- Peer review process
- Science communication to public

---

## 📋 Exemptions

PhD students who are **HKUST MPhil graduates** may be considered for exemption from this course, subject to the approval of their department.
