# PhD Qualifying Examination — Nano Sci & Tech (HKUST)

> **PhD Required | QE | NANO Postgrad Program**

---

## 📚 Overview

- **Program:** PhD in Nano Science and Technology
- **University:** HKUST
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD)
- **Grading:** Pass / Fail

---

## 🎯 QE Committee Structure

The qualifying examination is specified by the **Qualifying Examination Committee of the NANO postgraduate program**.

### Likely Committee Composition

- **Chair:** Senior faculty in Nano Sci & Tech
- **Members:** 2-4 faculty from related departments (Physics, Chemistry, Materials, Bio)
- **External examiner** (optional, in some cases)

---

## 📋 Requirements

- PhD students **must pass** a qualifying examination
- Typically within **first 2 years** of study (standard HKUST practice)
- Set by the QE Committee of the NANO program
- Pass required for PhD candidacy

---

## 🎯 Typical QE Components (interdisciplinary)

### 1. **Written Exam**
- Core nanoscience concepts
- Discipline-specific topics (chosen by committee)
- 2-4 hours duration

### 2. **Oral Exam**
- 1-2 hours
- Defense of research proposal
- Q&A on fundamentals

### 3. **Research Proposal**
- 5-15 pages
- Original research plan
- Literature review
- Methodology
