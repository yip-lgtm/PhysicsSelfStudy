## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** LANG 5000
- **Name:** Foundation in Listening & Speaking for Postgraduate Students
- **University:** HKUST
- **Program:** MPhil/PhD in Nano Science and Technology
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD Prep)
- **Credits:** 1
- **Grading:** P or F
- **Note:** Cannot be counted toward the credit requirements

---

## 🎯 Program Overview

### Target Audience
**For students whose level of spoken English is lower than ELPA Level 4 (Speaking)** when they enter the University.

### Course Description
The course addresses the **immediate linguistic needs of research postgraduate students** for oral communication on campus using English.

### Passing Requirement
Students are required to attain at least **ELPA Level 4 (Speaking)** to complete the course.

---

## 📋 ELPA Speaking Test

**Full-time RPg students are required to take an English Language Proficiency Assessment (ELPA) Speaking Test** administered by the **Center for Language Education** before the start of their first term of study.

### When LANG 5000 is Required

| Condition | LANG 5000 Required? |
|-----------|---------------------|
| ELPA Speaking Test ≥ Level 4 | ❌ No |
| ELPA Speaking Test < Level 4 | ✅ Yes (until pass) |
| Failed to take test in first term | ✅ Yes |

### Passing Requirement
Take LANG 5000 until pass by attaining at least **Level 4** in the ELPA Speaking Test before graduation.
