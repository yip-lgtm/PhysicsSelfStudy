## 🎯 Course Description

This course aims at equipping research postgraduate students with **transferrable skills** conducive to their professional development.

### Required Components

| Component | Hours | Notes |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Professional Conduct Training** | 3 hours | Mandatory |
| **Workshops (Self-Choice)** | 12 hours | 5 themes to choose from |

### Workshop Themes (5 categories)

Students complete **12 hours of workshops** at their own choice under the following themes:

1. **Communication Skills**
2. **Research Competency**
3. **Entrepreneurship**
4. **Self-Management**
5. **Career Development**

---

## 📋 Exemptions

PhD students who are **HKUST MPhil graduates** and have completed PDEV 6770 or other professional development courses offered by the University before may be **exempted**, subject to prior approval of the School.
