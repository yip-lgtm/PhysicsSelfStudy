# PHYS 6771 — Professional Development in Physics (HKUST)

> **MPhil/PhD Required | Nano Sci & Tech | Physics-specific professional development**

---

## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** PHYS 6771
- **Name:** Professional Development in Physics
- **University:** HKUST
- **Program:** MPhil/PhD in Nano Science and Technology
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD Prep)
- **Credits:** 1
- **Grading:** PP / P / F
- **Note:** Cannot be counted toward the credit requirements

---

## 🎯 Course Description

This **one-credit course** aims at providing **physics research postgraduate students discipline-specific trainings** such as:

- **Ethics**
- **Teaching skills**
- **Research management**
- **Career development**
- **Entrepreneurship**
- **Other professional skills**

---

## 📋 Requirements

- Required for MPhil/PhD in Nano Sci & Tech
- 1 credit (doesn't count toward credit requirements)
- Graded PP / P / F

---

## 📋 Exemptions

PhD students who are **HKUST MPhil graduates in Nano Science & Technology** and have completed:

- **PHYS 6770** Professional Development in Science (Physics), **or**
- **PHYS 6000** Physics Seminar, **or**
- **Other equivalent course(s)**

before may be **exempted** from taking PHYS 6771, subject to prior approval of the **Department Head or PG Coordinator**.
