## 🎯 Program Overview

The course is designed to **strengthen students' competence in teaching**. It comprises **2 parts**:

### **Part 1: 10-hour Mandatory Training (CEI)**

This 10-hour mandatory training course provides all graduate teaching assistants (GTAs) with the necessary theoretical knowledge with practical opportunities to apply and build up their knowledge, skills and confidence in taking up their teaching duties.

**At the end of the course, GTAs should be able to:**

1. **Facilitate teaching** in tutorials and laboratory settings
2. **Provide meaningful feedback** to their students
3. **Design an active learning environment** to engage their students

### **Part 2: Instructional Delivery**

Sessions assigned by respective departments. Formally assessed.

| Program | Sessions Required |
|---------|------------------|
| **MPhil** | At least **one 30-minute session** in front of a group of students for **one term** |
| **PhD** | At least **one 30-minute session** each in **two different terms** |
