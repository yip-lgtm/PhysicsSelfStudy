# Nano Science Technology PhD @ HKUST — Application Prep

> **Phase 4 | HKUST Nano Science & Technology PhD application materials**

---

## 🎯 PhD Program Overview

- **Program:** PhD in Nano Science and Technology
- **University:** HKUST
- **Duration:** 4 years
- **Funding:** HK$18,940/month (PhD Fellowship)
- **Cross-disciplinary:** Physics + Chemistry + Engineering + Biology

---

## 🎯 Research Areas

- **Nanomaterials synthesis**
- **Quantum devices**
- **Nanoelectronics**
- **Nanophotonics**
- **Biomedical nanotechnology**
- **Energy materials**

---

## 📋 Application Requirements

### Academic
- **Bachelor's/Master's** in Physics, Chemistry, Materials, EE, or related
- **GPA ≥ 3.5 / Second Upper Honor**
- **GRE** (recommended)
- **TOEFL/IELTS** for international students

### Documents
- Research statement (focus on nano)
- CV
- 2-3 recommendation letters
- Transcripts
