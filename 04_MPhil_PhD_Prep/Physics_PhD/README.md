# Physics PhD @ HKUST — Application & Program Prep

> **Phase 4 | HKUST MPhil/PhD in Physics — full program preparation**

---

## 🎯 Program Overview

- **Programs:** MPhil (2 years) + PhD (4 years) in Physics
- **University:** HKUST
- **Minimum credits:** 12 (MPhil/PhD)
- **Funding:** HK$18,940/month (PhD Fellowship)

---

## 📋 Program Requirements

### 1. **Minimum Credit Requirement**
- **MPhil/PhD:** 12 credits

### 2. **Required Courses**
- 12 credits of approved Physics postgraduate courses
- First two regular terms (full-time)
- Additional courses if first degree not in Physics

### 3. **Graduate Teaching Assistant Training**
- **PDEV 6800** (0 credits) — All full-time RPg required
  - 10-hour training by Center for Education Innovation (CEI)
  - MPhil: 1 × 30-min session in 1 term
  - PhD: 1 × 30-min session in 2 different terms

### 4. **Professional Development**
- **PDEV 6770** (1 credit, doesn't count) — Professional Development for RPg
- **PHYS 6771** (1 credit, doesn't count) — Professional Development in Physics
- Exemptions possible for HKUST MPhil graduates

### 5. **English Language Requirement**
- **ELPA Speaking Test** before first term
- **LANG 5000** (1 credit) — if ELPA < Level 4
- **LANG 5005** (1 credit) — Research Communication in English

### 6. **PhD Qualifying Examination**
- Within first **2 years** of study
- Set by the Department
- Pass required for PhD candidacy

### 7. **Thesis Research**
- **PHYS 6990** (MPhil Thesis Research, 0 credits) — MPhil
- **PHYS 7990** (Doctoral Thesis Research, 0 credits) — PhD (after QE)
- Oral defense required

## 🎯 Preparation Timeline (4-year plan)

### Year 1-2 (BSc Foundation)
- Strong physics foundation (PHYS 2124-4811)
- Maintain GPA ≥ 3.5
- Research projects

### Year 2-3 (MSc Data-Driven Modeling OR MSc Physics)
- Publish 1-2 papers
- Build research network
- Identify potential supervisors

### Year 3-4 (MSc Physics @ HKUST if not already)
- Take QE within 2 years of PhD start
- Publish 2-3 papers
- Find PhD supervisor
- Apply to PhD programs

### Year 4+ (PhD Application)
- Contact potential supervisors (Sep-Dec)
- Prepare research statement (Oct-Nov)
- Request recommendation letters (Nov)
- Submit applications (Dec)
- Interviews (Jan-Feb)
- Decision (Mar-Apr)
