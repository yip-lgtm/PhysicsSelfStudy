# Physics PhD @ HKUST — Application Prep

> **Phase 4 | HKUST Physics PhD application materials**

---

## 🎯 PhD Program Overview

- **Program:** PhD in Physics
- **University:** Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)
- **Duration:** 4 years (MPhil 2 years, then upgrade to PhD)
- **Funding:** HK$18,940/month (PhD Fellowship)
- **Areas:**
  - Astrophysics
  - Condensed Matter Physics
  - Particle Physics
  - Quantum Information

---

## 📋 Application Requirements

### Academic
- **Bachelor's degree** in Physics or related (with ≥3.5 GPA / Second Upper Honor)
- **GRE Physics** (recommended, not always required)
- **TOEFL ≥ 80** (or IELTS ≥ 6.0) for non-native English speakers

### Documents
- Online application form
- Research statement (1-2 pages)
- CV / Resume
- 2-3 recommendation letters
- Transcripts (all universities attended)
- English proficiency proof

### Timeline
- **Application deadline:** Usually December (for Fall admission)
- **Decision:** March-April
- **Start:** September

## 🎯 Preparation Timeline

### Year 1-2 (BSc Foundation)
- Build strong foundation in physics
- Maintain GPA
- Start research projects

### Year 2-3 (MSc Data-Driven)
- Publish 1-2 papers
- Attend conferences
- Build research network

### Year 3-4 (MSc Physics HKUST)
- Take qualifying exam
- Publish 2-3 papers
- Identify PhD supervisor

### Year 4+ (PhD Application)
- Contact potential supervisors
- Submit applications
- Interview
