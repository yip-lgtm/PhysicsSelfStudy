# PDEV 6800 — Introduction to Teaching and Learning in Higher Education (HKUST)

> **MPhil/PhD GTA Training | All full-time RPg required**

---

## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** PDEV 6800
- **Name:** Introduction to Teaching and Learning in Higher Education
- **University:** HKUST
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD Prep)
- **Credits:** 0 (required, non-credit)
- **Audience:** All full-time RPg (Research Postgraduate) students

---

## 🎯 Program Overview

### Components

1. **Training (10 hours)**
   - Offered by the Center for Education Innovation (CEI)
   - Teaching methodologies
   - Classroom management
   - Assessment design

2. **Instructional Delivery**
   - Sessions assigned by respective departments
   - Formally assessed

### Delivery Requirements

| Program | Sessions Required |
|---------|------------------|
| **MPhil** | At least **one 30-minute session** in front of a group of students for **one term** |
| **PhD** | At least **one 30-minute session** each in **two different terms** |

---

## 📋 Upon Satisfactory Completion

- Confirmed GTA training completion
- Required for graduation
- MPhil graduates with this completion can be exempted from similar requirements for HKUST PhD (subject to approval)
