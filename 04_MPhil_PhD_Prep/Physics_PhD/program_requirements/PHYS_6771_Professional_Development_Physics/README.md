# PHYS 6771 — Professional Development in Physics (HKUST)

> **MPhil/PhD Required | Physics-specific professional development**

---

## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** PHYS 6771
- **Name:** Professional Development in Physics
- **University:** HKUST
- **Department:** Physics
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD Prep)
- **Credits:** 1
- **Note:** Cannot be counted toward the credit requirements

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Physics-specific professional development covering:

- Research career paths in physics
- Academic job market
- Industry physics careers
- Grant writing
- Scientific publishing
- Conference presentations

---

## 📋 Exemptions

PhD students who are **HKUST MPhil graduates in Physics** and have completed:
- PHYS 6000 (Physics Seminar), **or**
- PHYS 6700 (Professional Development in Science - Physics)

before may be **exempted**, subject to prior approval of the Department Head or PG Coordinator.
