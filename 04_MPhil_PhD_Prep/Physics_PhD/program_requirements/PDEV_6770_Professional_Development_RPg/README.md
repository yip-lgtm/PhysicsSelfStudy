# PDEV 6770 — Professional Development for Research Postgraduate Students (HKUST)

> **MPhil/PhD Required | All RPg students**

---

## 📚 Course Information

- **Code:** PDEV 6770
- **Name:** Professional Development for Research Postgraduate Students
- **University:** HKUST
- **Phase:** 4 (PhD Prep)
- **Credits:** 1
- **Note:** Cannot be counted toward the credit requirements (12 credits minimum)

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Professional development for research postgraduate students covering:

- Research ethics
- Academic integrity
- Career planning
- Communication skills
- Leadership

---

## 📋 Exemptions

PhD students who are **HKUST MPhil graduates** and have completed PDEV 6770 or other professional development courses offered by the University before may be **exempted**, subject to prior approval of the School.
