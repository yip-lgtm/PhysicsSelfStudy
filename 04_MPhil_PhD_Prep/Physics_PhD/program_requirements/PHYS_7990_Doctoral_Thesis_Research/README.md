## 📋 PhD Thesis Structure

Typical PhD thesis (150-300 pages):
- Abstract (500 words)
- Introduction (extensive)
- Literature Review (comprehensive)
- Background Theory
- Methodology (multiple chapters)
- Original Research (3-5 publishable papers)
- Conclusion + Future Work
- References (Harvard format, 100+ sources)
