---

## 🎯 Program Overview

Under the **supervision of a faculty member**, students will carry out an **independent research project** on computational modeling and simulation.

---

## 🎯 Deliverables

1. **Independent Research**
   - Choose topic with faculty supervisor
   - Conduct original research
   - Apply computational modeling/simulation

2. **Short Thesis**
   - Written report
   - Document methodology + results
   - Literature review

3. **Oral Presentation**
   - Conference-style talk
   - Q&A session

---

## 📋 Requirements

- **Approval:** Course coordinator + supervisor
- **Duration:** Typically 1 semester
- **Topic:** Computational modeling or simulation

---

## 🎯 Project Ideas

- Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)
- Stochastic simulation of complex systems
- Network epidemic models
- Financial time series modeling
- Quantum simulation
- Materials simulation
