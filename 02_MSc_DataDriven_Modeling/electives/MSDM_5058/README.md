## 🎯 Learning Objectives

### 1. **Decision Theory (Finance)**
- Options and payoff diagrams (call, put)
- Binomial trees
- Wiener Process
- Ito Lemma
- **Black-Scholes-Merton Model**
- Greeks (delta, gamma, theta, vega, rho)

### 2. **Portfolio Management**
- Financial time series
- Mean-variance analysis (Markowitz)
- Modern portfolio theory
- Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)
- Risk metrics (VaR, CVaR)

### 3. **Evolutionary Computation for Optimization**
- Genetic algorithms (selection, crossover, mutation)
- Finding good prediction rules in finance
- Fitness functions
- Convergence analysis

### 4. **Information Theory**
- Measure of information
- Shannon entropy
- Various information entropies (Rényi, Tsallis)
- Methods of maximum entropy
- Mutual information
- KL divergence

### 5. **Nonlinear Time Series**
- Hurst exponent
- Multifractality in time series
- Detrending and decomposing
- Nonlinear models
- Long-range dependence

### 6. **Causality and Information Flow**
- Granger causality
- Transfer entropy
- Information flow measures
- Convergent cross mapping
- PCMCI (for time series)
