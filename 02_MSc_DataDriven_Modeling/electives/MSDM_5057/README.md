## 🎯 Learning Objectives

Build **essential business acumen** to equip students with the attitudes, skills and knowledge needed to thrive in today's data-driven world.

### Specialized Modules

1. **Management**
   - Leadership styles
   - Team dynamics
   - Project management

2. **Decision Making**
   - Decision frameworks
   - Risk analysis
   - Strategic thinking

3. **Business Communications**
   - Executive presentations
   - Stakeholder management
   - Persuasion techniques

4. **Collaborations**
   - Cross-functional teamwork
   - Conflict resolution
   - Negotiation

### Learning Format
- **Group work**
- **Industry expert exposure** (authentic learning experience)
- Case studies
- Real-world projects
