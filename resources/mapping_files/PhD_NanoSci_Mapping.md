## ⚠️ Important Distinctions

### **PHYS 6771 NOT NANO 6771**
- Despite being in the Nano Sci & Tech program
- The professional development course is **PHYS 6771** (cross-listed)
- This is consistent with Physics PhD program

### **Credit Transfer (PhD only)**
- Up to **6 credits** transferable from prior master's
- Must complete at least **3 NANO credits**
- Course selection must be approved by **Thesis Supervision Committee**

### **First 4 Terms (NOT 2 terms like Physics PhD)**
- Nano Sci & Tech has more flexible timeline
- Reflects cross-disciplinary nature of program
